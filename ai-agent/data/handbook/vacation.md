# Vacation and Time Off

Every full-time employee at Driftwood Labs gets 27 paid vacation days per calendar year. Part-time employees get a pro-rated amount based on their contracted hours.

Unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year, but only up to 5 days. Carried-over days expire if they are not used by March 31.

Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal, called Tidepool, at least two weeks in advance for any absence longer than three days. Shorter absences only need a heads-up to your team lead.

Driftwood Labs also closes completely between December 24 and January 1. These "quiet week" days do not count against your 27 vacation days.
