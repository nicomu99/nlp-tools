# Expenses

Work-related costs can be reimbursed through the Expenses tab in Tidepool. Submit expenses within 60 days of the purchase, otherwise they cannot be reimbursed.

Expenses under 75 euros are approved automatically. Anything 75 euros or more needs approval from your team lead, and anything over 1,000 euros also needs approval from finance.

Every expense needs a photo of the receipt, no matter how small the amount.

For travel, book trains instead of flights whenever the train journey is under six hours. Hotels are covered up to 150 euros per night, or 200 euros per night in London, Paris and Zurich.

Each employee also has a yearly learning budget of 1,200 euros for books, courses and conferences.
