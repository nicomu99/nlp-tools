# Laptops and Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. The laptop arrives at your home before your first day.

Laptops are replaced every three years. If yours breaks earlier, contact IT through the #it-help channel and you will get a loaner within two business days.

When you leave Driftwood Labs, you can buy your laptop for 100 euros if it is more than two years old. Otherwise it must be returned using the prepaid shipping box IT sends you.

Phones are not provided, but employees in the on-call rotation get 25 euros per month towards their phone bill.
