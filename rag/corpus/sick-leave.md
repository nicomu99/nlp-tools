# Sick Leave

If you are sick, tell your team lead before 10:00 on the first day you are out. A short message in the team channel is enough.

You do not need a doctor's note for the first three consecutive sick days. From the fourth consecutive day onward, upload a doctor's note to Tidepool.

Sick days are unlimited and are tracked separately from vacation. If you get sick during your vacation and have a doctor's note, those days are converted back into vacation days.

Mental health days are treated exactly like sick days. You never need to explain why you are taking one.
