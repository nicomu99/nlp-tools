# Security

All company accounts are protected with single sign-on and a hardware security key. You receive two YubiKeys on your first day: carry one and keep the other somewhere safe at home.

Never share customer data in chat. Use the encrypted vault in Tidepool if you need to send sensitive files to a colleague.

If you lose your laptop or a security key, report it in #security-incidents within one hour. Reporting quickly matters much more than reporting perfectly.

Phishing tests are sent at random a few times per year. If you spot one, forward it to phish@driftwoodlabs.example and you will earn a sticker.
