# The Lisbon Office

Our only physical office is in Lisbon, on the third floor of Rua das Gaivotas 12. It is open from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekdays.

Anyone can use the office, but desks must be booked in the Tidepool app the day before. There are 14 bookable desks and two quiet rooms.

The guest Wi-Fi network is called "Driftwood-Guest" and the password changes every Monday; it is posted on the whiteboard in the kitchen. Employees should use the "Driftwood-Staff" network, which logs in with your company account.

The espresso machine in the kitchen is named Bertha. Please descale Bertha when the orange light comes on; instructions are taped to the side of the machine.
