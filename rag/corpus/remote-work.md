# Remote Work

Driftwood Labs is remote-first. You can work from anywhere in a time zone between UTC-1 and UTC+3, so that every team has at least four overlapping working hours.

Working from outside that range is allowed for up to 30 days per year. Request it in Tidepool and get approval from your team lead.

Every employee receives a home office stipend of 40 euros per month for internet and electricity, paid automatically with the salary. On top of that there is a one-time setup budget of 600 euros for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment.

Core hours are 13:00 to 16:00 Lisbon time. Try not to schedule personal appointments during core hours.
