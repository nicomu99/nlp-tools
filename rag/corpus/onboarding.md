# Onboarding

Your first week at Driftwood Labs is called Harbor Week. You will not be expected to ship any work during Harbor Week; the goal is to meet people and set up your accounts.

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team. Your buddy will invite you to a 30-minute chat every week for your first two months.

On your first Monday there is a welcome call at 11:00 Lisbon time with the founders, Inês Moreira and Tobias Lund.

By the end of your first month you should have shipped one small change to production. Your team lead will help you pick a good first task.
