# On-Call Rotation

Engineers on the platform and payments teams take part in a weekly on-call rotation. A rotation starts every Tuesday at 10:00 Lisbon time and lasts exactly one week.

The on-call engineer must be able to respond to a page within 15 minutes, day or night. Pages come through the PagerTide app.

Each on-call week is compensated with 350 euros, plus one extra day off that must be taken within the following month.

If you need to swap your on-call week, arrange the swap with a colleague and update the schedule in PagerTide at least 48 hours before the rotation starts.
